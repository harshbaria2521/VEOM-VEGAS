IDEA TITLE
IDEA/SOLUTION: SVI — Smart Victim Intelligence
“From Listening to Meaningful Support”

SVI is an AI-powered real-time stress and trauma assessment module integrated with NHAA (14566) and its digital access channels. It analyzes voluntary interactions such as text, voice and user responses to identify indicators of emotional distress and trauma.

The proposed system will:
  • Perform real-time stress and trauma assessment using AI/ML.
  • Analyze textual and conversational signals to identify indicators such as distress, fear, anxiety, and hopelessness.
  • Use multimodal AI to combine linguistic, conversational and voice-based acoustic indicators.
  • Generate a non-diagnostic stress/trauma risk score and categorize cases into appropriate severity levels.
  • Provide early alerts and priority indicators to authorized support personnel.
  • Recommend appropriate support pathways (counselling, human intervention or emergency escalation).
  • Maintain privacy, consent and secure handling of sensitive information throughout.
Problem Resolution:
  • Detects emotional distress during the interaction itself.
  • Helps identify high-risk cases earlier.
  • Supports prioritization of cases requiring human attention.
  • Converts unstructured conversations into actionable assessment insights.
  • Enables consistent assessment across helpline and digital channels.
Unique Value Propositions:
  • Real-time AI-assisted assessment.
  • Multimodal stress & trauma analysis.
  • Early identification of high-risk cases.
  • Human-in-the-loop intervention.
  • Multilingual and accessible design.
  • Privacy-first and consent-driven architecture.
WORK FLOW
FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
Feasibility of the Idea:
  • Technical Feasibility: Uses established AI technologies including NLP, speech processing, transformer models and classification algorithms, making real-time assessment achievable.
  • Operational Feasibility: The module operates as an assessment layer over existing NHAA channels without requiring complete replacement of current infrastructure.
  • Scalability Architecture:
    14566 Helpline ➔ Web Portal ➔ Chatbot ➔ Mobile App ➔ IVRS
    API-driven microservices allow horizontal scaling to thousands of concurrent callers.
  • Economic Feasibility: Cloud-based deployment and open-source models reduce infrastructure costs while scaling dynamically with helpline call surges.
Potential Challenges, Risks & Overcomings:
  • AI Bias & Accuracy:
    Challenge: Emotional expressions vary across languages/cultures.
    ❖ Solution: Diverse datasets, continuous evaluation, fairness testing.
  • False Positives / False Negatives:
    Challenge: AI may incorrectly classify emotional states.
    ❖ Solution: AI as a decision-support system, with human review for high-risk cases.
  • Privacy & Data Security:
    Challenge: Victim conversations contain sensitive info.
    ❖ Solution: Consent-based collection, encryption, zero-storage option.
  • Multilingual & Dialect Variation:
    Challenge: Diverse Indian languages and regional dialects.
    ❖ Solution: Multilingual NLP/STT models with continuous tuning.
IMPACT AND BENEFITS
