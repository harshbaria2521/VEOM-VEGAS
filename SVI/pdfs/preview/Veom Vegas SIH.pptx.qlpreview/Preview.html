SOLUTION PIPELINE AT A GLANCE

STAGE 1: INGESTION & CONSENT

Multi-Channel Gateway (14566, Web, Chat, IVRS)

Acoustic Prosody + Transformer NLP

PROBLEM RESOLUTION (WHY SVI?)

✔ Real-Time In-Interaction Detection: Detects distress during the conversation itself rather than relying on delayed post-call questionnaires.

✔ Automated Priority Triage: Instantly bubbles high-risk and critical trauma cases to the top of the queue for human intervention.

✔ Unstructured to Actionable: Transforms emotional unstructured voice and text into standardized clinical telemetry for operators.

✔ Cross-Channel Parity: Ensures identical quality of stress assessment across 14566 phone lines, web portals, and mobile apps.

UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITIONS (UVP)

★ Multimodal Stress & Trauma Fusion: Combines vocal acoustic parameters (pitch, jitter, pace) with NLP conversational semantics for holistic risk scoring.

★ Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard: AI serves as a non-diagnostic decision-support assistant; human counsellors maintain full operational authority.

★ Privacy-First & Consent Architecture: Built with granular consent gates, in-memory stream processing, zero raw audio storage, and DPDP Act compliance.

★ Pan-India Multilingual Inclusivity: Seamlessly supports major Indian languages and regional dialects across digital portals and low-bandwidth IVRS.

POTENTIAL CHALLENGES, RISKS & MITIGATION ARCHITECTURE

⚠️ AI Bias & Regional Dialects

Emotional expressions and dialects vary widely across Indian languages.

🛡️ MITIGATION & SAFEGUARD

Diverse multilingual Indic datasets + continuous fairness testing + human validation.

⚠️ False Positives / Negatives

Risk of AI misinterpreting emotional trauma or distress level.

🛡️ MITIGATION & SAFEGUARD

Human-in-the-Loop decision support; AI recommends, authorized human counsellor decides.

⚠️ Victim Privacy & Data Security

Victim interactions contain deeply sensitive trauma and atrocity details.

🛡️ MITIGATION & SAFEGUARD

Consent gate, end-to-end encryption, ephemeral audio, zero raw voice retention (DPDP Act).

⚠️ Adversarial / Incomplete Data

Complainants may be unable or reluctant to articulate full details.

🛡️ MITIGATION & SAFEGUARD

Multi-signal cross-validation (vocal acoustics + semantic context + confidence scoring).
